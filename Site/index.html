Bouncer is a configurable coding-standards and code-smell checker. It reads TypeScript, JavaScript, Swift, Python and Go with real parse trees, everything else heuristically, and turns away what breaks your rules: as a pre-commit hook, a full-repo audit, or a coding agent's self-check.

$ curl -fsSL https://matthewa26.github.io/bouncer/install.ps1 | sh

One verified installer for every platform. Or brew tap matthewa26/tap && brew install bouncer.

How the door works

bouncer check

Checks IDs at the door

Runs on your staged files as a Husky pre-commit hook, or on the files an agent just edited, and fails the commit when a finding crosses your threshold.

bouncer review

Sweeps the whole club

Audits a whole repository, cross-file checks included: import cycles, layering, unused exports, duplicate code and git-history hotspots. Reports as console, table, JSON or HTML.

.bouncer

Your club, your list

Every rule, severity and threshold is configurable, per repo or globally. Save your choices as a profile and share it with your team.
